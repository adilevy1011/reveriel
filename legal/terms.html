Reveriel — Commission Request Process

These Terms of Service (the "Agreement") govern any commission accepted by the Artist ("Artist," "I," or "me") on behalf of a requesting party (the "Client," "you"). By submitting a commission request, remitting a deposit, or otherwise engaging the Artist's services, the Client agrees to be bound by the terms set out below.

1Commission Request Process

The Client shall submit a completed commission request form specifying the subject matter, preferred medium, dimensions, artistic style, reference materials, and desired completion date. Upon review of the request, the Artist will furnish a quote setting forth the applicable price and an estimated completion timeline.

Work shall commence only upon (a) the Client's approval of the quote, and (b) receipt of a non‑refundable deposit equal to fifty percent (50%) of the total commission price. The Artist will provide periodic progress updates throughout the commission to facilitate Client feedback and clarification.

2Copyright & Usage Rights

The Artist retains full copyright and all associated intellectual property rights in and to all commissioned artwork, unless commercial usage rights have been expressly purchased in a separate written agreement.

  1. Commissions made for personal use may not be reproduced, resold, sublicensed, or exploited for commercial purposes.
  2. The Artist reserves the right to display completed works — in whole or in part — within her portfolio, on social media, or for other promotional purposes, unless the parties have agreed otherwise in writing.

3Payment Terms

  1. A deposit of fifty percent (50%) of the total commission price is required prior to commencement of work and serves to secure the commission slot.
  2. The remaining balance is due in full upon completion of the artwork, following the Client's receipt of a watermarked preview.
  3. Final, unwatermarked digital files or physical deliverables will be released only after the Artist has received payment in full.
  4. Accepted payment methods include Venmo, PayPal, cash, or such other method as the parties may mutually agree upon.

4Revision Policy

Minor revisions — such as color adjustments or small, discrete detail changes — are included at no additional cost during the active creation phase of the commission. Major revisions or requests amounting to a substantive change in concept may incur additional fees, to be disclosed to the Client prior to implementation. Once the Client has approved the final watermarked version, only minimal adjustments may be made thereafter.

5Cancellation & Refunds

  1. Deposits become non‑refundable once work on the commission has begun.
  2. Should the Client cancel a commission after progress has started, no refund will be issued for work already completed.
  3. Should the Artist become unable to complete the commission, the Client will be entitled to a full or partial refund, calculated according to the stage of completion reached at the time of cancellation.

6Content Restrictions

The Artist reserves the right to decline any commission request involving hate speech, explicit sexual content, extreme violence, harassment, or material that infringes upon copyright or other intellectual property rights. The Artist may further decline, at her sole discretion and at any stage of the process, any request she finds personally objectionable.

  2. The Artist bears no obligation to verify such consent and disclaims all liability for any claim, dispute, or legal action arising from the Client's failure to secure proper authorization. The Client assumes full responsibility for the request and for any subsequent use of the resulting artwork.
  3. The Artist reserves the right to refuse or cancel, at her sole discretion, any commission involving a real individual, particularly where the request involves sensitive, sexual, defamatory, or otherwise inappropriate content.
  4. Commissions depicting minors will be accepted only upon receipt of clear, written consent from a parent or legal guardian of the minor. Under no circumstances will the Artist create sexualized or suggestive content involving minors.

The Artist bears no responsibility for the Client's use or misuse of the artwork following delivery, and shall not be held liable for delays arising from unforeseen circumstances, breakdowns in communication, or delayed feedback on the part of the Client.
