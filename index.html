Biography

Hi! I'm Sarah Ducheneaut, and I go by Reveriel online. I'm a visual artist from Sunnyvale, California, currently studying Studio Art and Psychology at UC Davis. My work grows out of a deep curiosity about how people experience the world—especially the quieter, often overlooked emotional moments that shape us. Through illustration and mixed media, I explore sensitivity, perception, and the space between feeling and form, with particular care for highly sensitive and neurodivergent experiences. I'm drawn to giving shape to feelings that don't always have names.
Outside the studio, I'm usually in motion—running cross country, hiking, or sailing around the Bay Area on the family Nonsuch 30. I love electronic music and will unapologetically nerd out as a certified house head. I'm also French, which explains my unpronounceable last name. Oh la la!

Commissions
